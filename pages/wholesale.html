Wholesale

Are you interested in becoming a wholesale partner with Rhino Fight Gear? 


We welcome the opportunity to collaborate with businesses that share our passion for high-quality martial arts gear. To qualify as a wholesaler, we require a minimum order value of $1000 excluding GST. 


Additionally, possessing a valid ABN or ACN number is necessary. 


As a valued wholesaler, we will provide you with a personalized price list that includes exclusive discounts.


Please note that our products should not be sold below the prices listed on our website to maintain a fair and supportive business environment.


Join us as a wholesaler and offer your customers the exceptional quality of Rhino Fight Gear while enjoying the benefits of a mutually beneficial partnership.

 

Contact us at nick@tubers.uk including your name, surname, company name, ABN or ACN number and phone number.
